Get a Permanent Free Link

To share this portal with candidates so it works everywhere, you need to host it. Here are the best 100% Free & Permanent methods:

Method 1: Netlify (Easiest)

1. Go to Netlify Drop.

2. Drag and drop your downloaded index.html file onto the circle.

3. They will give you a live link. Important: Click "Sign up" to create a free account right after to "claim" the site so it never expires!

Method 2: GitHub Pages (Best & Most Professional)

1. Create a free account on GitHub.com.

2. Create a "New Repository" (name it e.g., versant-portal).

3. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files" and upload your index.html.

4. Go to your repository Settings -> Pages (on the left menu).

5. Under "Build and deployment" -> "Branch", select main and click Save.

6. GitHub will give you a permanent link (like username.github.io/versant-portal) in about 2 minutes!
